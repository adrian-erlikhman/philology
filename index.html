Which language model wrote this text?

Paste a text. Philology compares it with 2,589 texts written by nine models and tells you whose writing it most resembles, how often that call has been right, and which parts of your text gave it away. It runs entirely in your browser; your text is never sent anywhere.

how it works

The verdict comes from a character n-gram classifier: it learns which short character sequences each model uses more or less than the others, then weighs those sequences in your text.

your text "…the model says, …" ↓ cut into every 1–4 character sequence "s" "sa" "say" "ays," "s, " … ↓ weight each one by tf-idf 40,000 numbers ↓ multiply by each model's learned weights one score per model ↓ softmax probabilities that add up to 1 verdict the model with the highest probability
